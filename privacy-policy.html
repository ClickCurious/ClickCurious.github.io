Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ClickCurious. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you visit and use our website.

2. Information We Collect

ClickCurious is designed to provide simple online tools. We do not currently require visitors to create accounts or provide personal information to use our main tools.

However, information may be collected automatically by hosting providers, browsers or third-party services. This may include technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, device information and access logs.

If you contact us, we may receive the name, email address and message you choose to provide.

3. How Our Tools Handle Your Information

Username Generator: Username suggestions are generated in your browser using JavaScript.

Discount Calculator: Calculations are performed in your browser. Values you enter are not intentionally sent to our server.

QR Code Generator: The QR Code Generator uses an external QR code service to create QR images. Information you enter, such as text or website links, is sent to that service to generate the QR code. Avoid entering passwords, confidential information or other sensitive data.

Shoe Size Converter: Conversions are calculated in your browser using built-in reference data.

Hashtag Generator: Hashtag suggestions are generated in your browser using built-in keyword lists.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ClickCurious does not currently require cookies for its main tools to function. However, our hosting provider or future third-party services, such as analytics or advertising providers, may use cookies or similar technologies.

We will update this policy if we introduce services that use cookies or collect additional information.

5. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on external services. These services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party providers when using their services.

6. How We Use Information

Information received through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, maintain website security, troubleshoot technical issues and improve our services.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information. However, no website or method of internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

ClickCurious is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website develops or our practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy practices, please visit our Contact Us page.

Note: This policy describes the website's intended current practices. It should be reviewed and updated before launch to reflect the actual hosting, contact, analytics, advertising and third-party services used by ClickCurious.
